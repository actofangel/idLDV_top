The Identity of Leonardo da Vinci

An independent research project grounded in primary sources. Its aim is to describe the works and thought of Leonardo da Vinci in a verifiable form, built on documentary records and physical observation.

Three kinds of statement

Every statement on this site declares which layer it belongs to.

Fact Documentary records, physical observations, content explicitly stated in a source

Interpretation Attribution, inferred intent, iconographic reading. Always accompanied by source references

Hypothesis The researcher's working hypotheses, stated together with supporting evidence and falsification conditions
